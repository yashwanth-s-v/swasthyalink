TOPIC: INTRODUCTION TO AYURVEDA

Ayurveda is a traditional system of medicine originating
in India.

Ayurvedic approaches may consider diet, lifestyle,
daily routines and individualized assessment as part
of health management.

Traditional Ayurvedic concepts include ideas such as
prakriti and dosha.

Ayurvedic assessment is individualized and should not
be determined solely from a short symptom description.

A person's symptoms do not automatically indicate a
specific dosha imbalance or disease.

A qualified Ayurvedic physician should perform a proper
assessment before recommending individualized treatment.

AI systems should provide educational information rather
than presenting themselves as Ayurvedic physicians.
