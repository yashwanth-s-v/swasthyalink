TOPIC: GENERAL FOOD AND LIFESTYLE GUIDANCE

A balanced diet, adequate hydration, regular physical
activity, sufficient sleep and a consistent daily routine
can support general wellbeing.

Individuals may notice that certain foods worsen symptoms.
Keeping track of foods and symptoms can help identify
personal triggers.

Eating very large meals may worsen digestive discomfort
for some people.

Highly processed foods and excessive consumption of
foods high in sugar, salt or unhealthy fats should
generally be limited as part of a balanced diet.

Food recommendations must account for individual needs.

People with medical conditions, food allergies, pregnancy,
or other special circumstances should seek appropriate
professional guidance before making significant dietary
changes.

General wellness advice should not be presented as a
treatment or cure for disease.
