TOPIC: HEALTH SAFETY

AI systems used in healthcare should not replace qualified
medical professionals.

Emergency symptoms require immediate professional
medical attention.

Potentially serious symptoms may include severe chest
pain, difficulty breathing, loss of consciousness,
severe bleeding, seizures, stroke-like symptoms or
other rapidly worsening conditions.

When serious symptoms are identified, the user should
be encouraged to seek appropriate urgent medical care.

AI-generated information should not be used to diagnose
disease or determine medication dosage.

Users should not be advised to stop, start or modify
prescription medication based solely on AI-generated
information.

When uncertainty exists, professional medical evaluation
should be preferred over automated guidance.
